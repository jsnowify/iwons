## Xor register: Proof strip

A switch register toggles each indicated punch position as the ribbon advances. Rafi rolls a fresh proof strip into the office typewriter. The supervisor wants the readings in their precise working order before signing the file.

Maintain a register initially 0. XOR each nonnegative reading into it and record the register after each reading.

Return the entire derived sequence, preserving its order.

Implement xorRegisterProofStrip(readings, k). k is part of the common inspection-card signature and does not affect this task's result.
