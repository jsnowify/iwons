# Learning-science implementation map

This is a design rationale, not evidence that Iwons improves learning. Automated correctness, accessibility and interaction tests cannot establish educational effects.

| Principle | Iwons mechanism | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retrieval practice | Guess slips, answer-before-reveal cards, Close the Book checkpoints | Correct answers can reflect short-term familiarity; delayed transfer is unmeasured |
| Spacing | Track/concept recall state, expanding day intervals, later session retry after a miss | The chosen intervals are product defaults, not an individually validated optimum |
| Interleaving | Planned deterministic Shuffle Drawer over finished material | Mixing unfinished concepts would be inappropriate; filtering is required |
| Worked examples and fading | Worked/completion/Parsons/fix/write rungs and optional scaffold suggestions | Two failures/clean solves are a simple heuristic, not a validated diagnosis |
| PRIMM | Predict, Run, Investigate, Modify, Make lesson steps | A solo browser tool cannot reproduce all classroom discussion in the research setting |
| Self-explanation | Optional Explain to Tik notes | No AI grading or inference of understanding from text length |
| Metacognition | Before-submit confidence and plain counts of confidence versus outcomes | Small samples are noisy; do not label learners overconfident or weak |
| Autonomy and competence | Optional next steps, reversible rungs, concrete feedback, recall/module rewards | XP is not a measure of ability or time spent learning |

The comparative review by [Dunlosky and colleagues (2013)](https://journals.sagepub.com/stoken/rbtfl/Z10jaVH/60XQM/full) supports practice testing and distributed practice across studied settings. It does not test this application. The [PRIMM study by Sentance, Waite and Kallia (2019)](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/229013/) studied school programming teaching; generalizing its results to independent first-year learners is an inference. [Ericson's research on adaptive Parsons problems (2018)](https://repository.gatech.edu/handle/1853/59890) motivates arranging code as a scaffold; Iwons must still verify its own task quality and keyboard interaction.

No VARK matching, intelligence labels, public rankings, punitive timers or claims that one fixed lesson sequence fits everyone. Learning events are local, exportable only by learner action, and separate from Watch integrity events.

## Not validated in Iwons

Delayed retention, transfer to unfamiliar programs, course completion rates, calibration improvement, optimal spacing, whether students stay engaged, native-quality Tagalog/Bisaya, and visual taste all require real learner/native-speaker evaluation. Separate review agents and rendered-page checks can find defects but cannot establish these outcomes.
